Ingredients:
	1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
	3 Tbs sesame seeds
	1/2 tsp dipping spices (30 turns)
	3 lb package of Rhodes Dinner Rolls (36 ct)
	1/4 cup margarine (melted)
	2 Tbs bacon crumbles (cherrywood)

Instructions:
	Thoroughly grease fluted pan.
	Combine cheese, seeds, and spices.
	Sprinkle 1/3 of mixture to coat sides and bottom of the pan.
	Place 15 rolls in pan.
	Drizzle 1/2 the margarine on rolls.
	Sprinkle with bacon.
	Place remaining 15 rolls in pan.
	Drizzle remaining butter.
	Sprinkle remaining mixture.
	Cover with plastic wrap and thaw in refrigerator overnight.
	Let pan stand at room temperature for 30 minutes.
	Bake uncovered at 350° for 20 minutes.
	Cover with foil and bake for another 15 minutes (until golden).
