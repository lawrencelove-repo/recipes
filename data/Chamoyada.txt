Title: Chamoyada
Description: Mango Raspa
Source: snapguide.com
Original URL: https://snapguide.com/guides/make-a-chamoyada-raspa/
Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
	3 Ripe mangos, fresh or frozen
	1 Cup Orange juice
	3/4 Cup Lime Juice
	Salt (to taste)
	Chili powder
	Chamoy sauce
	1/4 cup water
	1/4 tsp sugar

Instructions:
[Prepare raspa ]
	Peel mangos and place in blender.
	Add orange juice, lime juice, and 1 tablespoon of chamoy.
	Add 1 tsp of tajín.
	Blend well.
	Coat bottom of 4 plastic cups with chamoy.
	Evenly distribute blended mixture into the 4 cups.
	Place cups in freezer.

[Prepare to eat ]
	Dissolve 1/4 tsp of sugar into 1/4 cup of cold water.
	Put contents of one frozen cup into blender.
	Use Ice Crush setting on blender.
	Add water mixture to blender.
	Serve and eat.  Top with chamoy, if desired.
