Ingredients:
	2 to 4 center cut salmon filets
	1/3 cup peach preserves
	1 1/2 tsp siracha sauce

Instructions:
	Season filets with fresh ground salt
	Cook for 10 minutes, skin side down
	Brush on the sauce
	Flip and cook another 3 minutes
