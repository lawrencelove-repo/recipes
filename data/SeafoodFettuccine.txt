Title: Seafood Fettuccine
Description: 
Source: Epicurious
Original URL: https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/crawfish-etouffee-242926
Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
[Vegetables]
	1/4 cup chopped green bell peppers
	1/4 cup chopped onions
	1/4 cup chopped green onions (garnish)
	1/4 cup chopped celery (optional)

[Meats]
	2 cups peeled crawfish tails (12oz bag)
	1 cup of salad shrimp (4oz bag)
	1/2 cup crabmeat (6oz can Bumble Bee White Crabmeat)

[Sauce]
	2 sticks (16 tablespoons) butter
	1 tablespoon cornstarch
	1 1/2 cups warm water
	4 slices of American cheese
	1/4 tsp salt
	3/4 tsp cayenne pepper

Instructions:
	Finely chop vegetables and set aside.
	Cook pasta.
	Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the onions, bell peppers, and celery and cook, stirring, until the vegetables are soft and golden, 8 to 10 minutes.
	Add the crawfish tails and shrimp.  Cook, stirring occasionally, until they throw off some of their liquid, 6 to 8 minutes.
	Dissolve the cornstarch into warm water and add to the crawfish mixture. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until the mixture thickens, 4 to 5 minutes.
	Melt cheese into sauce.
	Season with salt and cayenne.
	Add crabmeat.
	Serve immediately over fettuccine pasta. Garnish with a sprinkling of green onions and parsley.
