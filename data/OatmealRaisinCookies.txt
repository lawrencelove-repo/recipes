Title: Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

Ingredients:
	1/2 cup vegetable oil
	1 1/4 cups sugar
	2 eggs
	1/3 cup molasses
	1 3/4 cups flour
	1 tsp baking soda
	1 tsp salt
	1 tsp cinnamon
	2 cups rolled oats
	1 1/2 cups raisins (1 cup purple, 1/2 cup gold)

Instructions:
	Heat oven to 400 degrees.
	Mix oil, sugar, eggs, and molasses.
	Stir in dry ingredients.
	Stir in oats and raisins.
	Place eight 1/2" balls on lightly greased baking sheets.
	Bake 13-15 minutes (watch carefully).
