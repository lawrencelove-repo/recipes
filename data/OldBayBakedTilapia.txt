Title: Old Bay Baked Tilapia
Description: 
Source: McCormick
Original URL: https://www.mccormick.com/blogs/old-bay-recipes/old-bay-baked-tilapia-recipe
Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
	1 pound tilapia fillets
	1/4 cup butter softened
	1 teaspoon OLD BAY® Classic Seafood Seasoning
	1/2 lemon
	1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley

Instructions:
	Preheat oven to 400°F. Place fish on parchment paper-lined 15x10x1-inch baking pan.
	Mix butter and OLD BAY in small bowl. Coat fish evenly on both sides with seasoned butter.
	Bake 10 to 15 minutes or until fish flakes easily with a fork. Squeeze lemon over fish and sprinkle with parsley before serving.
