Title: Accordion Potatoes

Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
	2 russet potatoes
	2 Tbsp vinegar
	1 Tbsp kosher salt

Instructions:
	Peel potatoes
	Slice into rectangles, keep in water until ready to cook
	Boil 96oz of water, adding 2 Tbsp vinegar and 1 Tbsp kosher salt
	Once boiling, add potatoes and simmer for 8 minutes
	Heat oil to 275° and fry for 6 minutes
	Remove from oil and let potatoes rest for 30 minutes
	Reheat oil to 375° and fry for 4 minutes
