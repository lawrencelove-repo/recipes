Title: Marinated Steak
Description: Dad’s recipe for marinated ribeye steay
Source: 
Original URL: https://m.wikihow.com/Marinate-a-Steak#Marinating_Steak
Yield: 
Active: .5 hr
Total: 4 hrs
Ingredients:
[Marinade]
	1/4 cup red wine (Cabernet Sauvignon)
	1/4 cup ketchup
	1/4 cup soy sauce
	1/2 cup vinegar
	3/4 cup water
	1/2 tsp garlic powder
	1 1/2 tsp MSG

Instructions:
[Marinating]
	Prepare marinade
	Cut nicks into steak
	Pour marinade over steak
	Allow meat to marinade for 3-4 hours in refrigerator
	Remove from marinade and allow meat to sit at room temperature for an hour
	For sauce, take remaining marinade, add 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and bring to a boil.

[Grilling]
	Preheat grill to 400°
	Lower burners to lowest setting
	Grill with cover closed for 3 minutes
	Grill with cover open for 2 minutes
	Flip
	Grill with cover closed for 3 minutes
	Grill with cover open for 2 minutes
	Cook to 150°
