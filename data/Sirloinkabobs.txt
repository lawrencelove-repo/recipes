Title: Sirloin kabobs
Description: 
Source: Foremangrillrecipes
Original URL: https://foremangrillrecipes.com/steak-recipes/easy-sirloin-steak/
Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
	2 – 8 ounce top sirloin steaks
	2 TBS extra virgin olive oil (EVVO)
	1 clove of garlic, minced or 1 tsp minced from bottle
	1/2 tsp course sea salt or Kosher salt
	1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Instructions:
	Put seasoned cubes on skewers
	Preheat the grill
	Spray grill with non-stick Pam
	Cook on grill for 2 minutes
	Flip over and cook 2 more minutes
