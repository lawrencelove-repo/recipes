Title: Mexican Rice
Description: 
Source: allrecipes.com
Original URL: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/241258/mexican-tomato-flavored-rice/
Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
	2 tablespoons vegetable oil
	1 cup long-grain rice
	2 cups water
	1/2 cup (4 oz) of tomato sauce
	1 1/3 teaspoons bouillon powder
	2 dashes onion powder
	2 dashes garlic powder

Instructions:
	Heat oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Fry rice in hot oil until golden brown, 2 to 3 minutes. Stream water into the saucepan while stirring the rice; season with tomato sauce, bouillon, onion powder, and garlic powder and stir.
	Bring the mixture to a boil and cook at a boil for 2 minutes. Reduce heat to low, place a cover on the saucepan, and cook until the moisture is mostly absorbed by the rice, about 15 minutes (on the right-hand rear burner). Remove saucepan from heat and let sit covered to let last moisture be absorbed into rice, about 5 minutes more.
	Add 5 minutes when doubling recipe.
