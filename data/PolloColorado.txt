Ingredients:
	1 whole chicken
	1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
	2 tbsp butter (softened)
	1 tsp granulated garlic
	1/2 tsp chili powder
	1/4 tsp paprika

Instructions:
	Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
	Clean chicken inside and out.
	Rub salt and butter on inside of chicken.
	Lightly sprinkle outside of chicken with dry ingredients.
	Place chicken breast side down in roasting pan and bake uncovered 40 minutes.
	Turn chicken over and return to oven for additional 40 minutes.
