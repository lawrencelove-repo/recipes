Title: Orange-Pineapple Bliss
Description: Makes 32 oz.
Source: 
Original URL: 
Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
	2 small oranges
	8 oz can pineapple chunks
	1/4 cup sugar
	1 cup lemon-lime soda
	6 ice cubes (3/4 cup crushed)
	1 small green apple (optional)

Instructions:
	Peel oranges and slice into six pieces each.
	Blend three pieces of oranges into blender with can of pineapple chunks.
	Blend on high.
	Mix in soda and sugar.
	Add last of oranges.
	Add peeled/sliced apple, if desired.
	Mix in ice cubes on medium.
