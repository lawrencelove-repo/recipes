Ingredients:
	1 loaf of French bread
	1 1/2 sticks of butter (12 Tbsp butter)
	1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
	4 Tbsp Parmesan cheese

Instructions:
	Slice it into 1 inch slices but not all the way through. Melt the butter.
	Add garlic powder and Parmesan cheese.
	Put about a Tbsp of the mixture on each side of the slice; I usually add the butter mixture on one side starting from one end, then I turn the bread & add the mixture to the other side.
	Bake at 375 for 20 minutes or so.
