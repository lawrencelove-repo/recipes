Ingredients:
	1 1/4 lb. peeled and deveined small shrimp (cooked or thawed)
	1/2 cup fresh lime juice (approx. 10-12 key limes)
	1 medium-sized red onion, peeled and rough chopped (1 cup)
	1/3 cup fresh cilantro leaves, chopped fine
	1/2 tsp garlic powder
	1/2 cup ketchup
	1 roma tomato (chopped)
	1/4 cup Valentina hot sauce (yellow bottle)
	2 tbsp avocado oil
	1 small ripe avocado, peeled, pitted and diced into small cubes
	1 tsp kosher salt
	fresh ground pepper (~20 half turns of peppermill)
	Tostadas or tortilla chips, for serving

Instructions:
[For the flavorings]
	Rinse the chopped onion under cold pouring water over a strainer, and then shake off any excess liquid. Add the onions into the shrimp bowl along with the lime juice, ketchup, hot sauce, tomato, cilantro, avocado oil, salt, ground pepper, and garlic powder. Do a taste test, and, if desired, add more salt to your tastes. Mix and stir everything together thoroughly.

[Prepping for Later]
	If you are not serving right away, cover the bowl and return it to the fridge.
	You can even keep the mixture and shrimp in separate containers and mix them right before serving.   This prevents the shrimp from “cooking” too long in the lime juice, which can make them rubbery.

[To Serve Your Ceviche de Camaron]
	Ladle the ceviche into small serving bowls; garnish with slices of lime and a few sprigs of cilantro. Serve with tostadas and/or tortilla chips as accompaniment.  Serve with avocado cubes.

Notes: Ceviche will taste the best when prepared early in the same day when it will be served. Just a few hours before serving is when you can add the flavorings to the shrimp.
