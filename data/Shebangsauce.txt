Title: Shebang sauce
Description: 
Source: 
Original URL: https://youtu.be/F2GP98-UKwI
Yield: 4 cups
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
[dry spices]
	2 Tbsp cajun seasoning
	2 Tbsp lemon pepper seasoning
	2 Tbsp old bay seasoning
	1 Tbsp paprika
	1 Tbsp cayenne pepper
	6 sticks salter butter
	2 cups minced garlic
	5 limes
	1 Tbsp brown sugar

Instructions:
	Melt butter in pan, over medium heat
	Add minced garlic.  Simmer for 4-5 minutes.
	Add dry ingredients.   Simmer to boil.
	Add brown sugar.   Heat till sauce thickens.
	Add lime juice.
