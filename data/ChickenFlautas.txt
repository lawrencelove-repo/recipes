Ingredients:
	2 cups shredded cooked chicken thighs (6 thighs)
	1 cup shredded Cheddar and Monterey Jack cheese blend (daiya dairy-free cheddar)
	2/3 cup Green salsa (Mrs. Renfro’s or La Costeña)
	1 teaspoon ground cumin
	6 flour tortillas (Tia Rosa - Fajita size)

Instructions:
	Boil chicken thighs for 25-30 minutes
	In medium bowl, stir together chicken, cheese, salsa and cumin; mix well. Spoon chicken mixture evenly down center of each tortilla. Tightly roll tortilla around filling; secure each with wooden pick.
	Heat deep fryer to 356°.  Cook 3 at a time for 3-5 minutes, or until golden brown.
	Serve flautas with desired toppings and Mexican condiments such as sour cream, guacamole and salsa.
