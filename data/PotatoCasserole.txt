Ingredients:
	2 lb package of hash browns (cubes)
	1 tsp salt
	1/4 tsp ground pepper
	2 tbsp dried minced onions
	1 can of cream of chicken soup
	16 oz pint sour cream
	2 cups grated mild cheddar cheese
	2 cups corn flakes (crushed)
	5 tbsp melted butter (optional)

Instructions:
	Mix all ingredients in bowl.
	Pour into 13x9 casserole.
	Cover with crushed corn flakes.
	Cover with foil.
	Cook in oven at 350 degrees for 40 minutes.
	Remove cover.
	Cook additional 40 minutes.
