Title: Deconstructed Sushi

Ingredients:
	1 salmon
	16 oz sushi rice
	2 mini seedless cucumbers (diced)
	2 Tablespoons olive oil
	3/4 cup panko breading
	1/2 cup kewpie mayonnaise
	1 tbsp sriracha sauce
	2 oz cream cheese (brick)
	rice sushi seasoning

Instructions:
[Preparation]
	Cook panko in olive oil over medium heat, until golden brown.
	Cook sushi rice.
	Season (garlic powder and salt) salmon and bake at 350° for 40 minutes (last 20 minutes at 425°).
	Cut each of two 1 oz slices of cream cheese into 8 pieces and roll in cooked (cooled) panko.
	Dice cucumbers into 1/8 inch cubes.

[Plating]
	Scoop rice into bowl.
	Slice and flake salmon.
	Season with rice seasoning and panko.
	Scoop cucumbers into bowl, along with cream cheese balls.
	Drizzle with spicy mayo.
	Add chili oil crunch (optional)
