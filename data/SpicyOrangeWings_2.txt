Title: Spicy Orange Wings
Description: 
Source: 
Original URL: 
Yield: 16-24 wings
Active: 30 min
Total: 40 min
Ingredients:
[Dry seasoning]
	1 T Lawry's seasoned salt
	1 tsp Lawry's seasoned pepper (heaping)
	1/4 cup cornstarch

[Spicy sauce]
	3/4 cup orange marmalade
	1 T brown sugar
	1 tsp hot sauce (rooster sauce)

[Misc.]
	2 lbs. chicken drumettes (approx. 16)
	2 T margarine

Instructions:
	Melt 1 Tbs margarine over medium heat in a large skillet.
	Combine salt, pepper, and cornstarch in a plastic bag (produce bags work best).  Add chicken and shake to coat.
	Add chicken, turn every 5 min for 25 minutes.  After 2 turns add the other 1 Tbs margarine.
	Mix marmalade, hot sauce, and brown sugar.
	After chicken is done, remove from pan, turn heat off, add sauce to pan to heat it up, then add chicken and coat with sauce.
	Serve with steamed rice.
