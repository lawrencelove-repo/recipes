Ingredients:
	3 eggs
	1 tsp cinnamon
	1/2 tsp vanilla extract
	1 Tbsp milk
	2 eggo waffles

Instructions:
	Mix ingredients
	Heat small frying pan over medium heat
	Lightly toast waffles
	Melt 1 Tbsp butter in frying pan
	Coat waffle in mixture
	Fry each side 2 minutes
	Sprinkle with powdered sugar
