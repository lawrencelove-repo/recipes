Title: Croissant Turkey Sandwiches

Ingredients:
	2 oz Sliced Turkey
	1 Croissant
	3 strips of bacon
	1 Roma tomato (sliced)
	Mayonnaise
	1 avocado (sliced)
	Green lettuce (leafed)

Instructions:
[Prep]
	Cook bacon in microwave.
	Preheat over 350 degrees.
	Slice the croissant in half.
	Bake the croissant on baking sheet for 5 minutes, stacked.
	Bake the croissant for 3 minutes, sliced-side down.
	Bake the croissant 1 1/2 minutes, sliced-side up.

[Assembly]
	Spread mayonnaise on both croissant halves.
	Place leafed lettuce on bottom half.
	Place 4 pieces of turkey on bottom half.
	Place 2 pieces of tomato on bottom half.
	Place 3 pieces of avocado on top half.
	Place 3 pieces of bacon on top half.
	Put the two halves together and slice in half.
