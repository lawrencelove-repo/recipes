Title: Frozen Pineapple Margaritas 
Description: 
Source: The Complete Savorist
Original URL: https://thecompletesavorist.com/frozen-pineapple-margarita
Yield: 2
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
	1 ripe pineapple (or 3 cups pineapple chunks)
	6 to 8 limes
	1/2 cup silver tequila
	1/4 cup agave nectar
	1/4 cup triple sec (orange liqueur)
	1/3 cup lime juice (with pulp)
	1 6 oz can pineapple juice

Instructions:
[Prep]
	Slice 1 pineapple into chunks and freeze (with juice)
	Squeeze 6-8 limes (save pulp)

[Blend]
	Add lime juice, tequila, triple sec, agave, and half of the pineapple to blender.
	Blend and then add remaining pineapple.
	Add can of pineapple juice and blend some more.
