Ingredients:
[brine]
	2 whole chickens
	2 caps full of chicken bouillon
	2 tubes of lemongrass paste

[spice base]
	3 heaping tbsp curry powder
	3 heaping tbsp coriander
	1/2 heaping tbsp tumeric

[additives]
	1 heaping tbsp creamy peanut butter
	1 square of trasi (shrimp paste)
	2 chopped white onion

Instructions:
[brine]
	Add water, chicken, bouillon, lemongrass to pot
	Boil uncovered till wings fall off (1 hour)
	Remove chicken
	Add peanut butter to water

[spice base]
	In skillet add 1 tbsp vegetable oil
	Heat oil
	Add onion
	Cook onion until clear
	Turn off heat
	Add coriander, tumeric, and curry to onions.  Mix.
	Add onions to broth.
	Ladle broth into skillet to get spices out.
	Pour spiced water into broth.
	Cook water for an hour.
	Check for flavor every 30 minutes
