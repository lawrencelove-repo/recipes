Ingredients:
	1 9oz can of La Colombe Mocha Latte
	1/2 cup whole milk
	1/2 cup Granulated Sugar
	1 cup Vanilla Ice Cream (3 scoops)
	2 cups Crushed Ice
	1 Tbsp chocolate syrup

Instructions:
	Mix together the crushed ice, coffee, milk and sugar to the blender and blend for 15 seconds.
	Add the ice cream and chocolate syrup, blend until smooth.
	Top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of cinnamon
