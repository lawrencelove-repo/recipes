Title: Banana Punch
Description: 
Source: Mom
Original URL: 
Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
[syrup]
	3 cups sugar
	6 cups water

[purée]
	7 bananas
	12 can frozen orange juice
	1 46 oz cap pineapple juice
	2 2L 7-Up bottles

Instructions:
	Dilute sugar in water to make syrup. Cut up bananas and blend in a blender.
	Add OJ to make the purée.
	In a bowl, add the sugar syrup and banana puree with pineapple juice.
	Freeze until slushy.
	Before serving, add 1 2L bottle of 7-Up to half of the frozen mixture.
