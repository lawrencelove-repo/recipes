Ingredients:
[Ribs]
	4 lbs+ rack of St. Louis Ribs

[Dry rub]
	1/4 cup brown sugar
	1 tbsp chili powder
	1 tsp paprika
	1/2 tsp celery salt
	1/2 tsp salt
	1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)

[Sauce]
	1/4 cup brown sugar
	1 tbsp chili powder
	1 tsp paprika
	1/2 tsp celery salt
	1/2 tsp salt
	8 oz can tomato sauce
	1/4 cup vinegar
	2 tbsp butter

Instructions:
[Boil ribs]
	Put ribs in a big pot
	Fill with water until it rises above the ribs
	Boil water at Medium heat
	Cook until meat loses its redness (approx. 8-10 minutes)

[Cook ribs]
	Preheat oven at 400°
	Remove from water and allow to cool
	Smother in dry rub
	Place on foil-lined baking sheet
	Cook uncovered for 15 minutes
	Flip ribs and continue to cook uncovered for another 15 minutes
	Flip ribs and baste with barbecue sauce
	Cover with foil and cook for 15 minutes

[Rotisserie ribs]
	Leave ribs at room temperature for 1 hour
	Preheat all 5 burners on lowest setting
	Remove membrane from rack of ribs
	Apply dry rub
	Insert rotisserie stake between 2nd and 3rd ribs
	Put stake into motor
	Turn off middle 3 burners, leaving only outermost burners on (roughly 250° for 1 rack 300° for 2 racks)
	Cook for 2 hours
	Baste with sauce and cook for 1 hour
