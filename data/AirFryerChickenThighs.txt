Title: Air Fryer Chicken Thighs
Description: 
Source: jenniferbanz
Original URL: https://jenniferbanz.com/air-fryer-chicken-thighs
Yield: 
Active: 
Total: 
Ingredients:
	4 Bone in, skin on chicken thighs,
	trimed of excess fat and skin
	1 teaspoon Smoked paprika
	1 teaspoon Garlic powder
	1/2 teaspoon Kosher salt
	1/2 teaspoon Oregano
	1/2 teaspoon Onion powder

Instructions:
	Preheat your air fryer to 380°F for 5 minutes. After pre-heating, spray with cooking spray.
	Add the chicken thighs to a large zip-top bag or a bowl with a lid. Mix the kosher salt, smoked paprika, garlic powder, oregano and onion powder and shake onto the chicken.
	Place the chicken thighs in the air fryer, skin side down, for 15 minutes.
	Turn your chicken thighs skin side up and cook for 12 minutes more. If you would like your chicken thighs super crispy. Cook for 2 minutes increments to get your desired crispness.
	Thighs are done when an internal meat thermometer reads 165°F.
	Serve.
